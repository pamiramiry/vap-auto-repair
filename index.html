Straight answers

Questions people ask before they call.

Question not here?

The quickest answer is a two-minute phone call.

(647) 710-0734
Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We find the problem, then we call you with what it is and what it costs. Nothing gets done until you say yes, and if we find something else along the way, we call again first.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend it. Booking ahead means your car goes straight into a bay instead of waiting in the lot. Call and we'll find a time that works.

Can I wait while you do an oil change?

Ask when you book. Routine jobs like oil changes and tire changeovers are what our four bays are set up for, and booking ahead is the best way to keep your wait short.

When should I book my winter tire changeover?

Early. The first snowfall in Toronto sends everyone to the shop in the same week. Book in October or early November and you'll skip the rush.

My check engine light is on. Is it safe to drive?

If the light is flashing, or the car is running rough, park it and call us. If it's steady and the car drives normally, it's usually safe to bring in, but don't leave it for weeks. A small fault can turn into a big one.

Will you try to sell me repairs I don't need?

No. We tell you what needs doing now and what can wait. If it can wait, we'll say so, and you decide.

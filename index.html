Will I know the price before you start?

Yes. We find the problem, then we call you with what it is and what it costs. Nothing gets done until you say yes.

Do I need an appointment?

We recommend it. Booking ahead means your car goes straight into a bay instead of waiting in the lot.

When should I book winter tires?

Early. The first snowfall sends everyone to the shop in the same week. Book in October or early November and you skip the rush.

My check engine light is on. Can I drive?

If it's flashing, or the car runs rough, park it and call us. If it's steady and the car drives normally, it's usually fine to bring in, but don't leave it for weeks.

Will you sell me repairs I don't need?

No. We tell you what needs doing now and what can wait, and you decide.
